Wedding & Cocktail Reception

★★★★★

The cocktail hour and reception are the perfect moments for close-up magic. I circulate among your guests in small groups, creating moments of astonishment and shared laughter. An original entertainment that breaks the ice and creates lasting memories for the newlyweds and their guests alike.
