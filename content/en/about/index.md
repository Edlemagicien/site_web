<h2 style="font-size:1.8rem; margin-bottom:1.25rem;">Hello, I'm Edgar</h2>
<p>
Magic fascinated me from a very young age, at a Christmas party event. A magician showed me the full extent of his powers. Curious and captivated, I threw myself into this world and never looked back!
</p>
<p>
Specialising in <strong>close-up</strong> and <strong>card magic</strong>,
I perform at the heart of your event: no stage, no curtain,
just you, me, and a few cards between our hands.
</p>
<p>
Every trick is a small story. Every reaction is my greatest reward.
After years of practice and refinement, I have had the privilege
of performing at weddings, corporate evenings and private events
throughout Île-de-France and beyond.
</p>
<h3 style="font-family: var(--ed-font-display); font-size:1.1rem; letter-spacing:0.08em; margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem;">My approach</h3>
<p>
I believe in magic that is elegant, sincere and respectful of the audience.
No magic wand, no smoke just the skill of the hands
and the shared pleasure of wonder.
</p>
